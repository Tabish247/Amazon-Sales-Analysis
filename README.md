# Sales Analysis Dashboard – Amazon Products

## Project Objective
The objective of this project is to analyze Amazon product sales performance using interactive visuals. The dashboard helps identify top-performing product categories, track monthly and weekly sales trends, and evaluate customer engagement through review data. It supports business decision-making by highlighting product growth opportunities and seasonal fluctuations.

## Dataset Used
The dataset includes Amazon product sales records with fields such as:
- Product Category
- Product Name
- Year-to-Date (YTD) Sales
- Quarter-to-Date (QTD) Sales
- Units Sold
- Customer Reviews
- Weekly and Monthly Sales Data

## Key Questions Answered
- What are the total YTD and QTD sales across all categories?
- Which product categories contribute the most to revenue?
- How do monthly and weekly sales trends behave across the year?
- Which products lead in YTD sales and customer reviews?
- What percentage of total sales is captured by each category?

## Process
1. Data Cleaning – Removed inconsistencies, formatted dates, checked nulls, and standardized numerical fields.
2. Data Transformation – Created YTD, QTD, and %Sales metrics; grouped data by month, week, and category.
3. Visualization – Developed interactive charts including line graphs, bar charts, and tables for multilevel insights.
4. Dashboard Design – Built a clean, KPI-driven layout with slicers for Product Category and Quarter.

## Key Insights
- Total YTD Sales reached $2.18M, with QTD Sales at $811K, showing strong quarterly momentum.
- Weekly sales show a progressive rise toward the year-end, indicating seasonal demand peaks.
- Camera and Car Accessories categories dominate with the highest % contribution to YTD revenue.
- Top products such as Nikon, Atomos, and SanDisk rank highly in both sales and customer reviews.
- Product categories like Mobile Accessories have low contribution and require strategic improvement.

## Final Conclusion
This dashboard delivers a complete overview of Amazon’s product performance, helping stakeholders understand sales patterns, top-profit categories, and consumer engagement levels. It serves as a powerful decision-support tool for planning marketing strategies, inventory management, and category-level growth actions.
